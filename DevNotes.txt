Domain Organization

- Core Metrics
    - GDP
    - Debt
    - Population
    - WealthGap
    - PovertyLine (dollar/day)
    - Social Benefits (% of population)
    - Literary Rate (% of population)
    - Life Expectancy
    - Infant Mortality
    - Relation Competing Powers
    
- Derive "absolute health metric" from core metrics
- Absolute metric must not go below 0.3 (Range 1...0)
- Core metrics are updated based on related "secondary metrics"

- "secondary metrics" are constantly updated based on policy decisions
- a "policy decision" can either add 0.01 or deduct 0.01 from a secondary metric
- the secondary metric will deduct or add a weighted equivalent to its related core metric
    - the actual influence of a secondary metric on a core metric is determined by the
        change amount (+/- 0.01) divided by the count of other secondary metrics that have influence on the core metric
- if a secondary metric reaches a total count of less than 0, it will have a rolling effect of a fixed --0.01 (not weighted) on the core metric

- Secondary Metrics
    - GDP
        - Unemployment Rate
        - Employable Population (% of total population)
        - Infrastructure Score (1...0)
        - Stability score : weighted based on (peace score, rule of law application score , corruption score)
        - Crisis Score : weighted based on crisis factors such as war, pandemic, corruption, unemployment rate, internal division
